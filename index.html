Why Oaxaca

I chose Oaxaca because I want to go there and it's an appropriate place to build this project because I'm interested in it. Before I leave, I want to know where I can stay and more. I want to know what the food is like, what's the history of the city, what are the markets, what are the places to visit which make the city so great?

What I would like to see.

The old fashioned history I can still see

The city of Oaxaca has historic streets, churches, museums and archaeological sites in the area. I enjoy the fact that I can discover various aspects of its history without having to spend the whole day in one museum.

Local food that is really local.Real local food.

I want to go to try mole, tlayudas, chocolate and quesillo. Also, I want to go to a market, not just to places geared towards tourists.

A trip that I am not in a hurry to get through.

I'd rather have time to look around, taste food and listen to the sounds of the world around me than to make a checklist of all the things I need to do to get it over with.
