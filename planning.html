I don't have to do it all

My biggest folly would be to do everything in one trip. I would prefer to pick some places that I really want to go to , and leave some time to work out how I'm going to get there before I begin making more plans.

The most urgent task I have to do

Before leaving

Have to verify transportation, housing, ID, payment and weather. I'd also store the significant addresses somewhere where I can get to without the web.

While there

I would stick to most days being very simple, a main destination, a time for meals and enough time to walk around. This is better than having to run all day long across the city.

First-trip priorities

  1. Don't fill the schedule, leave open time.
  2. Select a couple of meals and locations that I do care about.
  3. Check travel and accommodation arrangements.
  4. Begin with identification, money and safety fundamentals.

Planning update

I limited the project to Oaxaca to be able to focus on one destination and make it understandable.
