Part of the trip is eating!

The image is taken from a photo by Rafael Gutiérrez from Pixabay.

One of the things I'm really excited about Oaxaca for is food. I want to sample the foods that people are talking about, but I also want to see how the food fits into the daily routine, and not just consume it as a checklist of foods I need to eat.

The first thing I want to do is:

Mole

There are several regional versions of mole, and various variations in ingredients. I would not expect all of the plates to taste exactly the same, and that is why I'd want to try more than one.

Tlayudas

A tlayuda is prepared using a large tortilla that is toasted, beans and other ingredients. This would likely be one of the first things that I would have to order as it is local, simple and filling.

Chocolate and cheese

Oaxacan chocolate is usually made into a beverage, and quesillo is a string cheese that is utilized in many dishes. They both sound like they would be readily available at a market so they are already on my list.

A sample food day

If I selected one market stop, one main meal, and one drink my sample result would be:

Visiting the market in the morning, a tlayuda for lunch and oaxacan chocolate in the afternoon.

A basic rule

I would inquire about the cost before ordering, bring cash and watch the crowd at a food stand. I want to be able to have lots of options but not just spend irresponsibly
